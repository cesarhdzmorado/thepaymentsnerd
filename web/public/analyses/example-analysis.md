## The Shift Is Underway

Account-to-account (A2A) payments have been quietly gaining ground across Europe. What started as a regulatory push with PSD2 has evolved into a genuine market force, driven by merchant demand for lower interchange costs and consumer appetite for seamless experiences.

## Key Findings

**1. Volume growth is accelerating.** A2A payment volumes in Europe grew 23% year-over-year in 2025, with the Nordics and Netherlands leading adoption. The UK's open banking ecosystem processed over 12 billion transactions last year alone.

**2. Merchants are the primary demand driver.** Unlike card payments where issuer incentives drove adoption, A2A growth is being pulled by merchants seeking to reduce their cost of acceptance. Average savings of 40-60 basis points compared to card transactions make the economics compelling.

**3. The user experience gap is closing.** Early A2A implementations suffered from clunky redirect flows. New solutions leveraging variable recurring payments (VRPs) and embedded banking APIs now offer one-click checkout experiences that rival stored card payments.

## What This Means for the Industry

The implications extend beyond a simple shift in payment rails:

- **Card networks** are responding with lower interchange tiers and real-time settlement offerings
- **PSPs and acquirers** are racing to add A2A capabilities alongside card acceptance
- **Banks** face a strategic choice: become the A2A infrastructure layer or watch fintechs capture the value

## Looking Ahead

The next 18 months will be critical. The EU's PSD3 framework and the Instant Payments Regulation will further reduce barriers to A2A adoption. Watch for consolidation among A2A payment providers and partnerships between traditional acquirers and open banking platforms.

The question is no longer *if* A2A payments will reach scale in Europe, but *how fast* — and who captures the value when they do.
